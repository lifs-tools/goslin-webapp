Goslin · easter egg

Which effect should the logo get?

Each card runs live on an inline copy of the original logo. Click cards to select one or more; the preview at the bottom combines all three.

A · Blink now and then

Every few seconds, at a slightly random interval, the eye closes for a moment. Most visitors never notice; the ones who do smile. It needs no script and works even when the logo is a plain <img>.

CSS only

B · The eye follows the pointer

Move the mouse around the page: the eye glances towards the pointer, staying inside the head. It is the most visible of the three, so it may distract from the form.

small script

C · Click for a honk

Clicking the logo makes the gosling peck once and say Honk!, which only someone who clicks will find. Every fifth click it says something else.

small script
